Question: Outline the steps you took to get to your final CNN architecture and your reasoning at each step. Describe why you think the architecture is suitable for the current problem.

Answer: I used EfficientNet-B0 as the backbone. EfficientNet applies compound scaling (depth, width and input resolution are scaled together), which yields strong accuracy for a very small model (about 5.3M parameters). Because it was pretrained on ImageNet, its convolutional features already encode generic visual structure (edges, textures, object parts) that transfers well to landmark photographs, which are also natural images. I froze every backbone parameter and replaced the final classifier layer (classifier[1]) with a new nn.Linear(1280, 50), so only 64,050 parameters are trained. Training just the head is fast, memory-friendly (comfortably inside the 4 GB VRAM budget) and reduces overfitting on the ~4,000 training images, while still giving high accuracy thanks to the quality of the pretrained features.
